Sourcing & methodology

Incident data compiled from Wikipedia's List of railway accidents and incidents in India and the Ministry of Railways' consequential-accident statistics (published via dataful.in and indiastat.com). Coordinates are approximated at the town or station level for mapping — not precise crash-site locations. Fatality figures vary across sources, particularly for pre-1980 incidents; the most commonly cited figure is used here. This dataset covers major documented accidents and is not exhaustive — the Ministry of Railways recorded 38,500+ accidents between 1961 and 2019 alone, the vast majority minor.

Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_railway_accidents_and_incidents_in_India · dataful.in/datasets/1210 · indiastat.com
